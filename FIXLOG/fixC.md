# FIXLOG: Fix-C (tech review M1, M2, M3, M8, M10; minors 3 and 6)

All numbers: `results/fixc/FIXC.md` (generated by `scripts/gaps/fixc_report.py`), `results/published/CONTROLS.md`,
`results/large/tulu_controls.json`, `results/fp32scan/*.seedscan.json`, `results/gaps/*.membership3.blind.json`,
`results/controlled/weights_only*.json`, `results/fixc/n0*.fp32.json`, `results/gaps/swaps_v2.json`.
Freeze: git tag `fixc-frozen` (reader, swap test v2 rules A-D, membership method) before the fresh runs
n01-n06 (`scripts/gaps/runs_fixc.sh`) were trained.

## What changed in code

- `scripts/controlled/score.py`: the weights-only recipe is kept (`recipe_weights_only`), `n_from_data` is
  persisted, `n_source` says whether the reported step count came from the weights or from the claimed
  document list + batch size, `weights_only_ok`, `scope`; the seed search range is recorded
  (`seed.searched`, `seed.true_in_range`).
- `scripts/controlled/weights_only.py` (new): re-reads every run with `with_data=False`.
- `scripts/controlled/membership2.py`, `scripts/gaps/membership3.py`: `TF_BLIND=1` builds the step curve from the
  weights-only recipe instead of truth.json; one operating-point table (0, 0.1%, 1% FPR) for every attack with
  Clopper-Pearson intervals and held-out medians.
- `scripts/gaps/swaps.py`: also scans `runs/fixc`; reports seed confidence (`seed_hits / seed_of`). Rules A-D unchanged.
- `scripts/published/controls.py` (new): assumptions, cutoff x system-prompt sweep, and controls (cross-dataset,
  leave-out 1% / 10%, per-document power, extra-document power). Tokenization re-derived per document and checked
  against the stored counts: 0 differences in all 8 reference variants.
- `scripts/large/tulu_controls.py` (new): full 245-token list with counts, "changed at any level", and wrong-mixture
  cross-scoring for both Tulu stages.
- `scripts/fp32scan/s1_scan_full.py` (new): full seed x batch scan saved, bootstrap over tokens.
- `scripts/gaps/runs_fixc.sh`, `fixc_mem_chain.sh`, `fixc_report.py` (new).

## Corrected sentences (for README / article owners)

- Recipe (M1): "Kind, step count and warm-up come from the weights alone in __WO_OK__ of __WO_TOT__ pre-existing
  runs (no claimed dataset or batch size used); r05's step count (501 for 500) is the exception. The 24 runs use round
  hyper-parameters, seeds below 100,000 and one of the reader's five schedule families, and are single-epoch."
- Fresh runs (M1): "On five runs trained after the reader was frozen, with non-round learning rates and weight decay,
  random seeds and one schedule outside its five families, the reader recovered the full schedule in 2
  (n01, n04; peak within 1.5e-5), misread two with non-round weight decay (n03: decay 0.013 snapped to 0.015, warm-up
  18 for 17; n05: 738 steps for 500), and returned a wrong recipe for the out-of-family schedule (n02). Each wrong
  recipe is visible in the replay check: 621, 802,448 and 28 of about 24.4M never-updated weights bit-exact, against
  24,419,699 when the recipe is right. The direct token list had no false positives in any of the five."
- Seed (minor 6): "The seed search covers 0..99,999 plus a short list of common seeds. A random 32-bit seed (n01, n04)
  is outside it and is not found. Where the recipe was misread, placement fails and the seed with it (n02, n05);
  in n03 the true seed came first but reproduced only 10 of 490 placed documents' steps, too few to trust."
- Swaps (M3): "Rules frozen before training five fresh runs: the swap test was not usable on any of them, because the
  seed was not recovered with confidence (best seed reproduces 4-22 of the placed documents' steps). Without that
  gate it flags 2,314-2,558 clean documents per run. The earlier 27 / 40 with 0 false flags (s05) and 137 / 200 with
  7 false flags (five swap runs) hold for runs with round hyper-parameters and seeds below 100,000; 266 false flags when
  the step count is off by one (r05)."
- Membership (M2): "With the step curve read from the weights alone (not from the training log), at 0.1% false
